# SoMa West, San Francisco (audits in 2019 and 2020)

Block-by-block audits of the SoMa West Community Benefit District, run with the district and its residents: a community audit in August 2019 (22 volunteers) and a second audit from December 2020 to January 2021, plus cleanups in the area and reports from the QR codes Rubbish put on the district's Big Belly cans in 2020. Audits record street conditions as well as litter: graffiti, stains, tree wells, broken street furniture, needles and human waste.
