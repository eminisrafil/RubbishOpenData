# Cross-country drive (October 2019)

Adam Israfil of Rubbish drove from Philadelphia to California from 22 to 31 October 2019 and recorded the litter at each stop: Philadelphia, Cumberland MD, Indianapolis, St. Louis, Kansas City, Washington UT, Las Vegas and Bishop CA. Written up as "Cross Country Detrashing" (Nov 2019).

**File:** [`cross-country-2019-10.csv`](cross-country-2019-10.csv) · 1,538 records · 1,611 items · 2019-10-22 to 2019-10-31
