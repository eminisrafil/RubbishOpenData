# Events (2018 to 2026)

Litter recorded at conferences and community cleanups that Rubbish ran or supported: Startup Grind Global 2020 and 2022 (Fox Theatre, Redwood City), SXSW 2022, an APEC 2023 cleanup in San Francisco, an Earthday.org team in Flagstaff, a MOOP (leave-no-trace) project at regional events in Pennsylvania and New York, Sodus Bay cleanups, Crews & Brews in Westerly, Mountain View in 2018, and smaller cleanups.
