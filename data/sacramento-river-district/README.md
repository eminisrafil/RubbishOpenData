# River District, Sacramento (June 2021)

A three-day, street-by-street audit of the River District, Sacramento, from 6 to 9 June 2021, commissioned by the River District to document conditions across the district. Every issue seen was photographed and sorted by hand: litter, garbage piles, graffiti, tents and temporary shelters, needles, human waste and more.
