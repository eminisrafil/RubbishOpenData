# NoTraceTrails, Pacific Crest Trail survey (2023)

The raw app records of the NoTraceTrails survey of the whole Pacific Crest Trail in 2023, led by the Moore Institute for Plastic Pollution Research: a one-kilometre waste survey roughly every 16 km, each piece photographed and sorted by the surveyors in the Rubbish app (the app's AI was not used).

**File:** [`pacific-crest-trail-2023.csv`](pacific-crest-trail-2023.csv) · 913 records · 1,085 items · 2023-03-28 to 2023-09-24

The team's code: [wincowgerDEV/NoTraceTrails](https://github.com/wincowgerDEV/NoTraceTrails) (MIT).

## Notes

- The study's cleaned data and code are the reference version: McGruer et al. 2025, Waste Management 206, 115063 (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2025.115063); data and code at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15002831. Use those for analysis; this file is the app export behind them, limited to the survey itself, 28 Mar to Sep 2023 on the trail (a practice survey in Long Beach and one stray test record are left out).
- Please cite the paper when you use this set.
